    Questions we get before every project

    Something else on your mind? Ask it in your brief.

    How soon will the first leads come in?

    Ads can bring enquiries within days of going live. We use the first few weeks to test audiences, creatives and offers, then move budget to whatever brings genuine leads at the lowest cost.

    What budget do I need for ads?

    It depends on your city, industry and how many leads you need. We suggest a test budget after reading your brief. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google and is separate from our fee.

    Do you make the reels, or do I?

    Either works. We can script and shoot with you, edit footage you record on your phone, or bring in creators who film for you.

    Can you find influencers in my city or language?

    Yes. We shortlist creators by audience, location, language, content style and budget, then handle briefs, approvals, usage rights and reporting.

    Will I own the ad accounts and content?

    Yes. Ads run from accounts in your business's name, and you keep the pages, creatives and lead data we make for you.

    What happens after I send a brief?

    We read it, come back with questions or a short call, then send a scope and quote. There's no obligation to go ahead.

    What are you working on?

    Tell us about your business, your audience and what you'd like to achieve. Pick a starting point or just write to us.
